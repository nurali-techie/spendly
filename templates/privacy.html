Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account with Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Your name and email address used during registration.
  • Expense records you add, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Basic usage data such as login timestamps and session activity.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to operate and improve the Service:

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To display your expense history and spending summaries.
  • To respond to support requests or questions you send us.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app performance.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to providing the Service.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secured servers with access controls in place. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

However, no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your login credentials confidential.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • When required by law or in response to a valid legal process.
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Spendly or its users.
  • With service providers who assist in operating the app, under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below. We will process your request within 30 days.

You may also export your expense data from within the app at any time.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@spendly.app.
